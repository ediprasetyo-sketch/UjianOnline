V6.3.31 — Attempt Status Synchronization
- Active attempts are synchronized against the effective exam deadline.
- Effective deadline uses the earlier of attempt start + exam duration and exam end time.
- Expired attempts automatically change to status expired.
- Expired attempts are excluded from live participant monitoring.
- Dashboard and monitoring endpoint synchronize before reading statistics.
- Results page now separates Selesai, Waktu Habis, and Sedang Mengerjakan.
- Legacy incorrect deadline_at values are normalized during synchronization.
